# Data licensing

The licensing of the code in this repository is set out in `LICENSE` (MIT). The data files are licensed separately, as follows.

## Derived source data (this repository)

The derived tables in `source_data/` and `figure_data/` — interpolation evaluation, recovery ratios, bootstrap results, error decomposition, terrain fitting, information-source comparison, claim ledger, figure contracts, and the figure-source tables — were generated by the authors from the archived WRF fields. They are distributed under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)** license: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

## Natural Earth auxiliary data

The 110-m Natural Earth coastline and border data used by the study-design figure are bundled with the cartopy package and are in the **public domain** (Natural Earth: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/).

## Archived WRF dataset (not redistributed here)

The raw one-minute nested WRF fields are archived at the National Tibetan Plateau / Third Pole Environment Data Center (Feng and Li, 2026; DOI 10.11888/atmos.tpdc.303495). They are **not redistributed** in this repository because of size and third-party-use conditions; access and use are subject to the terms of the archived record. See `DATA_ACCESS.md`.
